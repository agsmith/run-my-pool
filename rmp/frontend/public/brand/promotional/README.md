# Run My Pool promotional logo assets

These PNG assets are lossless crops from the original `rmp.png` and `rmp2.png` presentation sheets in `../source/`.

## Primary logo family

- `rmp-horizontal-glow.png` — wide primary wordmark for dark banners and headers
- `rmp-stacked-lockup.png` — compact stacked wordmark for square or narrow placements
- `rmp-app-icon-framed.png` — framed app-icon treatment on a dark background
- `rmp-app-icon-light.png` — app-icon treatment on a white background
- `rmp-symbol-monochrome-dark.png` — white symbol on a dark background
- `rmp-symbol-checkerboard.png` — symbol over the checkerboard shown in the source sheet
- `rmp-compact-lockup-circuit.png` — centered compact wordmark on the circuit background

## Alternate logo family

- `rmp-alt-horizontal-command-center.png` — wide alternate wordmark on the command-center background
- `rmp-alt-stacked-command-center.png` — vertical alternate lockup on the command-center background
- `rmp-alt-app-icon-framed.png` — alternate framed app icon
- `rmp-alt-horizontal-dark.png` — alternate horizontal wordmark for dark placements
- `rmp-alt-horizontal-lime.png` — lime alternate horizontal wordmark
- `rmp-alt-monochrome-light.png` — monochrome alternate wordmark on the light checkerboard
- `rmp-alt-compact-dark.png` — compact alternate wordmark on dark
- `rmp-alt-compact-light.png` — compact alternate wordmark on white

## Production note

The two source files are flattened raster presentation sheets, not layered originals. Their backgrounds—including the checkerboard pattern—are baked into the pixels. For transparent-background, print-scale, or vector assets, obtain the original SVG, EPS, PDF, or layered design files from the logo designer rather than attempting to remove these backgrounds automatically.
